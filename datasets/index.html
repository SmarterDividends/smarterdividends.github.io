Open data

Raises, decreases,
and the context.

Two free CC BY 4.0 files contain detected dividend changes. These are event snapshots, not every historical payment or a complete market census.

  • Dividend raises CSV
  • Dividend cuts CSV

Source files refresh daily and begin with a # metadata line. Skip that line when parsing. A database outage can produce a header-only response; reject empty downloads.

Variable distributions can appear as increases or decreases. UAN is one example. A decrease is not automatically a corporate dividend-policy cut. Dates can be future ex-dates for announcements.

Column dictionary

ColumnMeaning
ex_dateEx-dividend date, including announced future dates.
tickerU.S.-listed ticker.
companySecurity name.
sectorSector classification; may be missing.
amount_beforePrevious per-share distribution in USD.
amount_afterNew per-share distribution in USD.
pct_changeFraction, not percent: -0.3617 is -36.17%.
streak_yearsGrowth streak recorded with the event.
safety_score_beforeRecorded model safety score. Timing is not guaranteed to be point-in-time.
safety_grade_beforeA/B/C/D/F grade derived from the recorded score.
detectedDate the event was detected.
